Privacy Policy

Local LLM: On-Device · Effective November 2, 2026

Local LLM is an iPhone app that runs language models on your device. It does not require an account. We do not operate a backend that stores your chats.

Data we do not collect

We do not create user accounts, run ads, or include analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. We do not sell personal information.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Chats and attachments. Conversations, photos, files, and generated replies are stored in the app’s local database on your device.
  • Model weights. Models you download are saved on the device for on-device inference.
  • Settings. Preferences such as language and generation options stay on the device.

Deleting the app removes this local data, subject to iOS backups you have enabled.

Apple Shortcuts. When you run a Local LLM action in Shortcuts, the text you choose is passed to the app and the completed answer is returned to Shortcuts. The app opens to process the request with your selected local model. Any later actions you add to your shortcut determine where that result is sent or saved.

Permissions

The app asks for access only when you use the related feature:

  • Photo Library — show recent photos in the add menu and attach them to a chat. Access is read-only; the app does not save photos back to your library.
  • Camera — take a photo and attach it to a chat, or provide camera frames to the on-device model during Live camera conversations.
  • Microphone — voice input in the composer and Live voice mode.
  • Speech Recognition — turn speech into text. The app prefers on-device recognition. If it is unavailable or a local recognition attempt fails, Apple’s speech service may process audio when online recognition is allowed. This fallback is enabled by default and can be turned off in the app’s Privacy settings; changes apply to the next recording. Apple processes this audio under its own privacy policy.
  • Notifications — optional alerts, for example when a model download finishes.

You can change these in iOS Settings at any time.

When the app goes online

Network access is used for:

  • Model catalog. The model list is fetched from a public GitHub JSON file so the catalog can update without an app update. Catalog artwork and model logos may also be loaded from their hosting services, including GitHub and jsDelivr. These requests do not include your chats.
  • Model downloads. When you choose a model, downloads use ModelScope (modelscope.cn) for mainland China network IPs and Hugging Face (huggingface.co) elsewhere. Before downloading, the app contacts Country.is (api.country.is), or MyIP (api.myip.com) if needed, to identify the country of your network IP. These services receive your IP address; the app does not save it or request device location. If region lookup fails, downloads can still proceed; a supported alternate host may be tried after a connection failure. These requests do not include your chats.
  • Web Search (optional). When you enable Web Search or ask the app to read a link, the app may send search queries or page URLs to our search gateway, hosted on Cloudflare, which calls Tavily for search and Jina for page text. Queries may be derived locally from your message and relevant conversation context. Earlier versions may use DuckDuckGo and read websites directly. Information included in queries or URLs may therefore leave your device. The complete chat transcript, photos and attachments are not uploaded by these search interfaces. Model-generated answers are still produced on device. Web Search stays off until you enable it.

Custom connections (optional). If you select a custom search provider in Settings, search queries go directly to your Tavily, Brave or Firecrawl account instead of our managed gateway. Page links are sent to Tavily or Firecrawl for reading; with Brave, the app reads the website directly. Usage is charged under your provider account. API keys and optional Hugging Face access tokens are stored in this device’s Keychain and are not synced by the app. Hugging Face tokens are sent only to huggingface.co, not to download mirrors. Removing a key stops new requests from using it; requests already in progress may finish.

Search service protection. Our gateway stores an App Attest public key and request counter to verify an app installation, and limited usage counters to enforce search allowances. It temporarily processes IP-derived rate-limit identifiers and one-time challenges. It does not store query text, page content or chat logs. Network hosts and search providers process requests under their own policies.

Weather queries. When Web Search is enabled and you ask about weather, the app may send the requested city to Apple’s geocoding service and use the resulting city coordinates to request Apple Weather data through WeatherKit. The city may be taken from your message or resolved locally from earlier messages. This does not request your device’s current location.

Network providers and websites receive information needed to serve a request, such as your IP address and the requested resource. They may retain request information under their own policies. We do not receive a copy of your chats from them and do not control their retention or deletion practices. See the policies of Apple, DuckDuckGo, GitHub, Hugging Face, jsDelivr, Cloudflare, Tavily, and Jina. Other websites you access have their own policies.

Downloading a third-party model does not send your conversations to its publisher for inference. Optional web search and online speech recognition are separate network features described above.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.

隐私政策

Local LLM: On-Device · 生效日期 2026年10月17日

Local LLM 是一款在 iPhone 本机运行大语言模型的应用，无需账号。我们没有用于保存你对话记录的服务器后台。

我们不收集的数据

我们不提供用户账号，不投放广告，也不内置统计分析或崩溃上报 SDK。我们不出售个人信息。

保留在本机的数据

  • 对话与附件。 聊天、图片、文件和生成内容保存在本机应用数据库中。
  • 模型权重。 你下载的模型保存在本机，用于端侧推理。
  • 设置。 语言、生成参数等偏好仅保存在本机。

删除应用会清除上述本地数据（若你开启了 iOS 备份，备份中可能仍有副本）。

Apple 快捷指令。 当你运行 Local LLM 的快捷指令操作时，所选文字会传入应用，完成的回答会返回快捷指令。应用会打开并使用你选中的本机模型处理请求。你在快捷指令中添加的后续操作决定结果发送或保存到哪里。

系统权限

仅在你使用对应功能时才会请求：

  • 相册 — 在加号菜单显示最近照片并附加到对话。仅读取，不会把照片写回相册。
  • 相机 — 拍照并附加到对话，或在 Live 相机对话中将画面提供给本机模型。
  • 麦克风 — 输入框语音输入，以及 Live 语音模式。
  • 语音识别 — 将语音转为文字。应用优先使用设备端识别；若不可用或本机识别尝试失败，在允许联网识别时，音频可能由 Apple 语音服务处理。此回退选项默认开启，可在应用隐私设置中关闭，更改将在下次录音时生效。Apple 按其隐私政策处理这些音频。
  • 通知 — 可选，例如模型下载完成提醒。

你可以随时在 iOS 设置中关闭这些权限。

何时会联网

  • 模型目录。 从公开的 GitHub JSON 拉取模型列表，无需发版即可更新目录。目录封面和模型图标也可能从其托管服务加载，包括 GitHub 和 jsDelivr。这些请求不含你的对话内容。
  • 模型下载。 你选择下载时，中国大陆网络 IP 使用魔搭（modelscope.cn），其他地区使用 Hugging Face（huggingface.co）。下载前，应用通过 Country.is（api.country.is），必要时通过 MyIP（api.myip.com），查询网络 IP 所属国家或地区。上述服务会收到你的 IP 地址；应用不保存该地址，也不请求设备定位权限。地区查询失败不会阻止下载；连接失败时可能尝试支持该模型的备用下载源。这些请求不含你的对话内容。
  • 网页搜索（可选）。 开启网页搜索或要求应用读取链接时，应用可能将搜索词或网页地址发送到我们托管于 Cloudflare 的搜索网关，再由 Tavily 搜索、Jina 提取网页正文。搜索词可能在本机结合当前消息和相关对话生成。较早版本可能使用 DuckDuckGo 并直接读取网站。因此，搜索词或链接中包含的信息可能离开设备。这些搜索接口不会上传完整对话、照片或附件，模型回答仍在本机生成。网页搜索默认关闭，需你主动打开。
  • 自定义连接（可选）。 如果你在设置中选择自定义搜索服务，搜索词将直接发送到你自己的 Tavily、Brave 或 Firecrawl 账户，不经过我们的默认搜索网关。网页链接由 Tavily 或 Firecrawl 读取；使用 Brave 时，应用直接读取网站。使用费用计入你的服务商账户。API 密钥和可选的 Hugging Face 访问令牌保存在本设备的系统钥匙串中，应用不会同步这些凭据。Hugging Face 令牌只发送给 huggingface.co，不发送给下载镜像。删除密钥后，新请求不再使用它；已开始的请求可能继续完成。

搜索服务保护。我们的网关保存 App Attest 公钥和请求计数以验证应用安装实例，并保存有限的使用量计数以执行搜索额度限制。网关临时处理由 IP 派生的限流标识及一次性验证值，不保存搜索词、网页正文或对话日志。网络托管商和搜索服务商按其各自政策处理请求。

天气查询。开启网页搜索并询问天气时，应用可能将查询城市发送给 Apple 地理编码服务，再使用解析出的城市坐标通过 WeatherKit 请求 Apple 天气数据。城市可能来自当前消息，或在本机从此前消息中解析。这不会请求设备的当前位置。

网络服务商和网站会收到处理请求所需的信息，例如 IP 地址和请求的资源，并可能按其自身政策保留请求信息。我们不会从他们那里拿到你的对话副本，也无法控制其保留或删除方式。请参阅 Apple、DuckDuckGo、GitHub、Hugging Face 、jsDelivr、Cloudflare、Tavily 和 Jina 的政策。你访问的其他网站也有各自的政策。

下载第三方模型并不意味着将对话发送给模型发布方进行推理。可选的网页搜索和联网语音识别是上文单独说明的联网功能。
