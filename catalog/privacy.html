Privacy Policy

Local LLM: On-Device · Effective August 21, 2026

Local LLM is an iPhone app that runs language models on your device. It does not require an account. We do not operate a backend that stores your chats.

Data we do not collect

We do not create user accounts, run ads, or include analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. We do not sell personal information.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Chats and attachments. Conversations, photos, files, and generated replies are stored in the app’s local database on your device.
  • Model weights. Models you download are saved on the device for on-device inference.
  • Settings. Preferences such as language and generation options stay on the device.

Deleting the app removes this local data, subject to iOS backups you have enabled.

Permissions

The app asks for access only when you use the related feature:

  • Photo Library — attach photos from your library to a chat.
  • Camera — take a photo and attach it to a chat.
  • Microphone — voice input in the composer and Live voice mode.
  • Speech Recognition — turn speech into text. The app prefers on-device recognition. If that is unavailable, Apple’s speech service may process audio under Apple’s privacy policy.
  • Notifications — optional alerts, for example when a model download finishes.

You can change these in iOS Settings at any time.

When the app goes online

Network access is used for:

  • Model catalog. The signed-off model list is fetched from a public GitHub JSON file so the catalog can update without an app update.
  • Model downloads. Weights are downloaded from Hugging Face (huggingface.co) when you choose a model.
  • Web Search (optional). If you turn on Web Search for a message, the app queries DuckDuckGo and may fetch short excerpts from result pages to ground that reply. The local model still generates the answer on device. Web Search is off unless you enable it.

Those third parties process the request according to their own policies. We do not receive a copy of your chats from them.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.

隐私政策

Local LLM: On-Device · 生效日期 2026年8月21日

Local LLM 是一款在 iPhone 本机运行大语言模型的应用，无需账号。我们没有用于保存你对话记录的服务器后台。

我们不收集的数据

我们不提供用户账号，不投放广告，也不内置统计分析或崩溃上报 SDK。我们不出售个人信息。

保留在本机的数据

  • 对话与附件。 聊天、图片、文件和生成内容保存在本机应用数据库中。
  • 模型权重。 你下载的模型保存在本机，用于端侧推理。
  • 设置。 语言、生成参数等偏好仅保存在本机。

删除应用会清除上述本地数据（若你开启了 iOS 备份，备份中可能仍有副本）。

系统权限

仅在你使用对应功能时才会请求：

  • 相册 — 从相册选取图片并附加到对话。
  • 相机 — 拍照并附加到对话。
  • 麦克风 — 输入框语音输入，以及 Live 语音模式。
  • 语音识别 — 将语音转为文字。应用优先使用设备端识别；若不可用，音频可能由 Apple 语音服务处理，适用 Apple 的隐私政策。
  • 通知 — 可选，例如模型下载完成提醒。

你可以随时在 iOS 设置中关闭这些权限。

何时会联网

  • 模型目录。 从公开的 GitHub JSON 拉取已审核模型列表，无需发版即可更新目录。
  • 模型下载。 你选择下载时，权重来自 Hugging Face（huggingface.co）。
  • 网页搜索（可选）。 仅在你为某条消息打开网页搜索时，应用会查询 DuckDuckGo，并可能抓取结果页的短摘录作为该次回复的依据。回答仍由本机模型生成。默认关闭。

上述第三方按其自身政策处理请求。我们不会从他们那里拿到你的对话副本。

儿童

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，我们也不会故意收集儿童的个人信息。

变更

若本政策更新，我们会修改本页顶部的日期。
